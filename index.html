18summers Privacy Policy

18summers keeps everything on your iPhone. It has no account and no server, and it makes no network connections.

Your kids' names, ages, photos, answers, voice recordings and the ideas you save are stored on your device, in the app's own storage. Your iPhone backup (iCloud or your computer) includes them, like any app's data.

The app collects no data, uses no analytics or advertising, and shares nothing with us or anyone else.

With your permission it reads the calendars on this iPhone to suggest ideas that fit; a plan you add to your calendar becomes a normal event and syncs like any other. It uses the microphone only while you record.

If you export your memories, the file goes only where you choose to send it. Deleting the app deletes its data from this phone.
